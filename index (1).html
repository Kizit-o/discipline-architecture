How the 90 days work

Foundation. Desert. Integration.

One calendar runs everything. Day 1 begins when you sign the contract with a witness, not before.

Days 1 to 30

Foundation

Score your baseline in the audit. Design three micro-disciplines. Run a dry run, lock on Day 22, then run the daily stack.

Days 31 to 66

Desert

Results are mostly invisible. You track completion, not results. If completion falls below 80%, you halve the action and keep the cue.

Days 67 to 90

Integration

Keep the three micro-disciplines running, re-measure, and run the Day 90 assessment that sets your next cycle.

Day 90 is a checkpoint, not a finish line. With the lock on Day 22, you will have about 68 days of repetition. One study of 96 people found an average of 66 days for a new behavior to become automatic, with a range from 18 to 254 days across people and behaviors. A slower pace is a result to read, not a verdict on you.

Questions

Before you buy.

How much time does it take each day?

The audit takes about 90 minutes, once, in week one. After that the structured part of a day is about 20 minutes across three check-ins, and the Sunday review takes 20 minutes. These are the author's estimates. Time yourself and record the real numbers.

Do I need your other books?

No. The Core Guide holds the frameworks and protocols in full, and the Tracker holds everything you fill in. Nothing here depends on an earlier IMPOSSIBLE title.

Can I use it on my phone, or do I need to print it?

The Core Guide is sized for a phone screen. The Tracker is A4. Fill the fillable edition on a computer or tablet in Adobe Acrobat Reader, or print the print edition and write by hand. Phone readers handle form fields inconsistently, so a phone is the weakest way to fill it.

Do not open the PDFs in Word. Use Adobe Acrobat Reader, which is free.

Does it work? Where is the evidence?

The audit is self-scored and has not been validated, so use it to compare your own before and after, not to compare yourself with anyone. Version 1.0 has not yet been tested on a group of users. Treat your first 30 days as the test. The Core Guide marks every claim as established, inferred, or a design decision.
